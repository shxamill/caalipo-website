# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful and constructive place to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Assume good intent and explain disagreements calmly.
- Keep discussions welcoming to people with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may restrict participation when necessary. For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately through https://github.com/shxamill.
